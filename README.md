# TalentBridge — Candidate Recruitment Portal

A clean, transparent recruitment tracking portal designed for candidates — so you always know where you stand, what's next, and what you need to prepare.

---

## What Is This?

Most recruitment processes are a black box from the candidate's side. You apply, maybe get a call, and then wait — with little visibility into which stage you're at, what documents are needed, or when to expect the next step.

**TalentBridge Candidate Portal** solves that. It gives every candidate a personalised dashboard that tracks their entire recruitment journey in one place — from the moment they apply to the day their laptop arrives on their desk.

---

## The Portal at a Glance

Once logged in, a candidate sees:

- **Where they currently stand** in the recruitment process
- **What stage is active** and what's coming up next
- **Interview details** — date, time, platform, interviewer, and duration
- **Documents required** and which ones have been submitted
- **Assets being prepared** for their joining day
- **A full activity log** of every communication and milestone

---

## Recruitment Stages Tracked

The portal tracks the candidate's progress across 10 stages, shown as a live visual pipeline:

**Stage 1 — Application Received**
Confirms the application was submitted and received successfully.

**Stage 2 — Resume Screening**
Shows whether the profile has been reviewed and shortlisted by the recruitment team.

**Stage 3 — Recruiter Discussion**
Covers the initial call with the recruiter — role expectations, location, and CTC discussion.

**Stage 4 — Technical Round 1**
First technical interview. After completion, the result (Cleared / Pending / More Rounds) is updated here.

**Stage 5 — Technical Round 2**
Architecture or advanced technical panel. Same result visibility as Round 1.

**Stage 6 — HR Discussion**
Cultural fit, salary negotiation, and final offer discussion with the HR lead.

**Stage 7 — Pre-Onboarding**
Background verification, offer letter acceptance, and pre-joining form completion.

**Stage 8 — Document Submission**
A checklist of all required documents — educational certificates, PAN, Aadhaar, experience letters, and more. Candidates upload directly through the portal. Items unlock automatically after the offer is accepted.

**Stage 9 — Onboarding**
Day 1 orientation details, team introductions, compliance training, and system access provisioning schedule.

**Stage 10 — Asset Delivery**
Tracks every IT asset being prepared for the candidate — laptop, employee ID card, access badge, corporate email, SAP system credentials, and welcome kit — each with an expected delivery date and current status.

---

## Document Checklist

The portal maintains a real-time checklist of all documents required for joining:

- Updated Resume / CV
- Offer Letter Acceptance
- Educational Certificates
- Experience Letters (previous employers)
- PAN Card
- Aadhaar Card
- Address Proof
- Medical Fitness Certificate

Each document shows one of three states: **Submitted**, **Pending Upload**, or **Locked** (unlocks at the appropriate stage).

## Activity Log

A chronological feed of every update in the recruitment process — recruiter calls, interview outcomes, document requests, and upcoming actions — so candidates have a complete record of their journey.

---

## Why This Matters

Recruitment is stressful enough without having to chase updates. A portal like this respects the candidate's time, reduces back-and-forth emails, and builds trust in the organisation before the candidate even joins. Transparency at this stage reflects directly on the company culture they are about to enter.
